The Logic: "Percentage Error"

Calculate how far a city's actual numbers deviate from your ideal targets on a percentage basis. A lower total deviation means a higher Match Score.

The "Small Number" Effect:

Adjust small percentages gradually. If you target 2% Unemployment and a city has 4%, that counts as being 100% off (double your target). Small deviations in rates (poverty, unemployment) can impact the score more heavily than large dollar amounts (income, home price).
